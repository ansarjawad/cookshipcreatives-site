The method

A loop, not a delivery schedule.

Every round of testing feeds the next round of research. Nothing gets produced without a reason attached to it.

01

Research

Reviews, Reddit, Amazon, your own ad account. What customers actually say, not what we assume.

02

Strategy

Personas ranked by how strongly people feel, mapped against every angle and awareness stage you're not reaching.

03

Produce

Concepts built to fill a named gap. Every asset ships tagged: persona, angle, why it exists.

04

Test & prove

Hook rate first, cheap and fast. Cost per result second, once it's earned the spend. Winners and losers both write back to research.

↻ Back to 01. A winner fatigues in weeks, not months — the loop is the product, not a phase you finish.

Fair questions

Straight answers.

Do you only do video?

For now, yes, deliberately. It's where the sharpest, most common gap sits across the accounts we've researched, and we'd rather be genuinely good at one range of formats than thin across all of them on day one. Static and founder-content direction are on the roadmap as we take on capacity.

Is AI involved in production?

Yes, and it's disclosed — Meta requires it on any AI-assisted ad. What we won't do is generate a synthetic person claiming your product gave them a personal result. That's the one thing audiences reliably punish, and it's a hard line for us regardless of the format.

How do you know what to make?

Reviews — including the bad ones — Reddit threads, Amazon's own buyer-persona data, your ad account's real history. Competitors come last in our process, never first: leading with a competitor scan just means arriving at the idea they already had.

Do you run our ads?

No, and we don't want to. We build the creative and design the test; your buyer executes it. That's also why our guarantee sits on hook rate, not ROAS — hook rate is the thing our work actually controls.

What do you guarantee?

Within 90 days, one concept beats your current best ad on hook rate, or the next month is free. We agree the benchmark from your own account data before any work starts, so there's no argument later about what "current best" meant.

What's the contract?

90 days standard. Long enough to test properly. Short enough that we have to earn the renewal every quarter.

Why should we trust an agency with no case studies?

You shouldn't, blindly — check the work instead. Everything on this page traces to something public: the Meta Ad Library, our published teardowns, the research standard above. Founding clients get a rate reflecting exactly this stage, in exchange for read-only ad-account access and permission to publish real results once they exist.
