Ad we love: ECHO, hearing earbuds 👂

"I'm 71 and I'll tell you straight, five years of saying what?"

Before the product appears, before a single benefit is named, this ad has already told you who it is for. A granddaughter in a yellow dress whispers into her grandfather's ear. He frowns, nods and pretends. His friend laughs. Every grandparent watching knows that nod, and every grandchild knows what it hides.

AD BREAKDOWN

The market: people in their seventies whose hearing is going and whose pride will not let them say so, and the families who have learned to repeat themselves in silence.

The desire: love and companionship, and beneath it a wish to be free of fear and pain. Nobody in this ad wants better hearing. The man wants to be in the room with the people he loves. No copy created that wish. The ad does the one thing an ad can do, which is to gather a desire already present in the market and point it at a product.

The awareness: the viewer is problem aware. He knows what is wrong, so the ad spends no time explaining it. It shows him the price instead: a secret missed, a wife's voice at dinner lost under the noise, an empty school hall he dreads walking into.

The moment: a whisper he can only pretend to hear. Quiet sadness and shame, turning to warm joy, in public, among the people whose opinion matters most to him.

The turn: the obstacle is pride, and the ad treats it as pride. A friend on the dock calls him proud and hands him a tiny case. Nobody sells him anything. He is given permission.

The proof: rain on the roof, a red cardigan, his wife's laugh bringing tears. The ad never claims that the product works. It lets the viewer hear what he hears.

The payoff: at the school play, from the back of the hall, "my grandpa is my hero."

The product: it arrives late, and it is sung. No doctor's visit, fits in your pocket, nobody can tell. Each line answers one reason for waiting, and by then the viewer has stopped looking for reasons.

The promise: one tiny pair of earbuds, and you will not miss another word from the people you love.
