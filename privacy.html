Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what data cookshipcreatives.com ("the site") collects, why, and the choices you have. The site is run by Cookshipcreatives, an AI animation studio. Questions: hello@cookshipcreatives.com.

What we collect

The site has no sign-up, no forms and no checkout of its own. We collect:

  • Usage data: pages you view, buttons you click (for example "Book a meeting", "Get ads like this" or a package button), videos you play, the site or link that brought you here, and general device, browser and approximate location (country/city) information. This is collected through the analytics and advertising tools listed below, using cookies and similar technologies.
  • What you send us: if you email us, message us on WhatsApp or book a call, we receive what you share there (such as your name, email, phone number, brand and message).

How we use it

  • To see what works on the site and improve it.
  • To measure our marketing and show our ads to people who showed interest.
  • To reply to you and work with you when you contact us.

How long we keep it

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Messages and project information you send us are kept while we work together and for a reasonable time after, unless you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a private window.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Control ad personalisation in your Google, Meta and X account settings, or at aboutads.info.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete the data you sent us: email hello@cookshipcreatives.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, and we'll help you use them.

Children

The site is meant for businesses and isn't directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
